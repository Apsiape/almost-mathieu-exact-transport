\begin{abstract}
We determine the transport exponents and packing dimension of the almost Mathieu operator $(Hu)(n)=u(n+1)+u(n-1)+2\lambda\cos 2\pi(\theta+n\alpha)\,u(n)$ for every $\lambda>1$ and every irrational $\alpha$. Let $\beta(\alpha)=\limsup_n q_n^{-1}\log q_{n+1}$ and $\gamma=\max\{0,1-\log\lambda/\beta(\alpha)\}$ ($\gamma=0$ if $\beta(\alpha)=0$, $\gamma=1$ if $\beta(\alpha)=\infty$). For almost every phase, every moment of a packet started at one site has upper growth exponent $\gamma$, and so does its fastest polynomial tail. Transport thus switches on at the spectral transition $\lambda=e^{\beta}$ of Avila, You and Zhou. The lower bound holds at every phase. If $\beta(\alpha)>\log\lambda$ it is carried by a fixed fraction of the packet: for $c>h>\log\lambda$ and large $n$ with $T=|q_n\alpha-p_n|^{-1}\ge e^{cq_n}$, a fraction at least $2^{-32}/(1+4\pi\lambda)$ lies beyond distance $T^{1-h/b_n}$, $b_n=q_n^{-1}\log T$; these exponents tend to $\gamma$ as $b_n\to\beta(\alpha)$ and $h\downarrow\log\lambda$. For almost every phase, every nonzero spectral measure has upper local dimension $\gamma$ almost everywhere, so both its packing dimensions equal $\gamma$; at every phase they are at least $\gamma$. For $0<\log\lambda<\beta<\infty$ this makes the packing bound $2(1-\log\lambda/\beta)$ of Jitomirskaya, Liu and Tcheremchantsev sharp with factor $1$, for almost every phase. The proofs use an exactly conserved polynomial of $H$, an arcsine law for the rational discriminant, and long elliptic runs of the Weyl solution. As $\beta(\alpha)$ can be noncomputable for computable $\alpha$, so can $\gamma$: at $\lambda=2$, $\theta=1/4$, the exponents realized by computable frequencies with eventually odd denominators are exactly the reals in $[0,1]$ that are right-computably enumerable relative to the halting problem, and one explicit computable operator has exponent of Turing degree $\mathbf 0''$.
\end{abstract}
